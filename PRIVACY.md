# BrightSteps privacy notice — development version

BrightSteps is currently a development prototype. It is not approved for collecting or processing children's personal, health, diagnostic, educational or therapy records.

## What this prototype stores
- On-device mode stores profiles, preferences, learning activity counts, planner items and notes in the browser's local storage on that device.
- If the optional Supabase vault is configured by the project owner, an authenticated user can save a client-side encrypted backup. Supabase receives ciphertext and cryptographic metadata, not the readable profile book.
- The website does not currently provide parent/guardian consent, teacher or therapist collaboration, role-based learner sharing, clinical records management or a production support process.

## User responsibilities
Use nicknames and demonstration data only. Keep local device access protected. Export and securely store backups. Protect the separate encryption passphrase; losing it prevents recovery of cloud backups. Sign out on shared devices.

## Before public child use
The operator must publish a legally reviewed privacy notice identifying the data controller, lawful basis, purposes, data categories, recipients, hosting locations, retention periods, deletion process, contact details and applicable rights. Obtain valid parent/guardian consent where required and seek the child's assent in an age- and communication-appropriate way. Complete a privacy impact assessment, security review, safeguarding review and jurisdiction-specific legal review. This document is not legal advice and is not a production privacy policy.
