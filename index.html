Adaptive learning guidance

  • If an activity feels difficult, offer a different explanation, example or way to respond.
  • If attention fades, offer a choice, movement or a pause without pressure.
  • Allow processing time; avoid repeating prompts too quickly.
  • Use the learner's interests without making access to preferred activities conditional on compliance.
  • Ask the learner what helps and respect communication such as “no”, “stop” or “break”.

Important

BrightSteps is an educational prototype, not a diagnostic tool or clinical treatment. Learning suggestions are simple demo rules, not AI assessment. Do not enter sensitive personal or medical information. For a real deployment, child accounts, professional access, consent, security, accessibility and educational content need specialist review.
